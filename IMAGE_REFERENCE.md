## 📐 Image Specifications

### Recommended Sizes

| Image Type | Minimum Width | Recommended Width | Aspect Ratio |
|------------|--------------|-------------------|--------------|
| Logo | 200px | 300px | Variable |
| Hero/Featured | 1200px | 1920px | 16:9 |
| Product Cards | 800px | 1200px | 4:3 |
| About Section | 800px | 1200px | 4:5 |
| Heritage Cards | 800px | 1200px | 3:2 |
| Testimonials | 300px | 600px | 1:1 (square) |

### File Formats
- **Logo**: PNG (with transparency)
- **Photos**: JPG (optimized)
- **Graphics**: PNG or SVG

### File Size Optimization
Before uploading, compress images:
- **TinyPNG**: https://tinypng.com
- **Squoosh**: https://squoosh.app
- **ImageOptim** (Mac): https://imageoptim.com

**Target sizes:**
- Logo: < 100KB
- Product images: 200-500KB
- Lifestyle images: 200-800KB
- Testimonial avatars: < 150KB

## 🎨 Image Style Guide

For a cohesive, premium look, all images should:

### Color Palette
- **Warm tones** - Browns, golds, greens
- **Natural lighting** - Soft, diffused light
- **Earthy backgrounds** - Wood, fabric, natural materials

### Mood & Feel
- ✅ Premium and elegant
- ✅ Natural and organic
- ✅ Warm and inviting
- ✅ Authentic and trustworthy
- ❌ Not overly corporate
- ❌ Not sterile or cold
- ❌ Not cluttered or busy

### Composition
- Clean, uncluttered backgrounds
- Focus on the product/subject
- Rule of thirds
- Depth and dimension
- Natural, candid moments (for lifestyle shots)

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Consistency is Key**
   - Use similar lighting across all product images
   - Maintain consistent color grading
   - Use the same background style

2. **Mobile Optimization**
   - Images will be cropped on mobile
   - Keep important elements centered
   - Test how images look on small screens

3. **Retina Display Support**
   - Use 2x resolution for crisp display
   - Example: 800px display → use 1600px image

4. **Alt Text** (Already included in HTML)
   - Screen readers use alt text
   - Good for SEO
   - Describes image content
